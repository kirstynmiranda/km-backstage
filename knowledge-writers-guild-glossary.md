# Vinted glossary — seed list

## How to use this list

This is a list of seed jargon and abbreviations that SC 3.1.3 (Unusual Words) and SC 3.1.4 (Abbreviations) rely on. This is a starting list — content-design must validate, correct, and extend.

- The agent should consult this glossary whenever it evaluates a passage of copy for SC 3.1.3 or SC 3.1.4.
- If the copy uses a term listed as "Expand on first use" and this appears to be the first use in the flow, raise a `Minor` finding at `AAA` conformance, source `WCAG 2.2 SC 3.1.3 AAA; Vinted glossary: [term]`.
- If the copy uses an internal-only term (marked "never user-facing" above) in a user-facing surface, raise a `Major` finding.
- Content-design team: correct, expand, and reprioritise this list. Terms without an agreed plain-language equivalent should be added with `TBD` so we can flag gaps.

## General terms

The following terms are used frequently in UI copy and other product-related content across all areas and domains. 

* **Parcel \-** Use “parcel” to refer to Avoid using synonyms, like “package”   
* **Transaction \-** Used to describe the interaction between a seller and a buyer once an item has been purchased. Transactions begin when a member purchases an item and end once the seller gets paid for the purchase.   
* **Member \-** Anyone who is signed in at Vinted is a “member.” Never use “user,” “customer,” or “client.”   
* **Seller \-** A member who sells an item is referred to as the seller for the duration of the transaction. A transaction ends when the seller gets paid for the purchase.   
* **Buyer \-** A member who buys an item is referred to as a “seller” for the duration of the transaction.   
* **Item \-** Anything that members list on Vinted is called an “item.” Don’t use “product”

## Domain-specific terms and abbreviations

For each entry:

- **Term** — the string as seen in the product
- **Kind** — jargon / abbreviation / Vinted-specific
- **Plain-language equivalent** — what to say to a first-time user
- **Notes** — context, caveats, and when the term may be acceptable unexpanded

### Marketplace and listing

| Term | Kind | Plain-language equivalent | Notes |
|------|------|---------------------------|-------|
| Bundle | Vinted-specific | A group of items from one seller, bought together | Differs from everyday "bundle". Define on first use in flows. |
| Catalog | Vinted-specific | Your list of items for sale | Also used for the browse tree. Context-dependent. |
| Wardrobe | Vinted-specific | The items a user has on sale | Sometimes used interchangeably with "catalog". Pick one and stay consistent (SC 3.2.4). |
| Counter-offer | Jargon | A reply offer at a different price | Familiar to people who've used marketplaces; explain once in negotiation flow. |
| Offer | Jargon | A proposed price | Generally understood; flag if confusable with promo "offers". |
| Listing | Jargon | An item for sale | Widely understood. |
| Drop | Jargon | Reduce the price | Context-dependent; "drop the price" is plain. |
| OBO | Abbreviation | Or best offer | Fashion-marketplace shorthand. Expand on first use. |
| Pro | Vinted-specific | Professional seller account | Capitalised "Pro" is Vinted-specific. Define on first mention. |
| Verified | Vinted-specific | Seller has verified their identity | Expand: "Verified — we've checked this seller's ID". |
