# Vinted voice and tone - general guidelines

## 1. Brand foundation

### 1.1 Company Context
* Company name: Vinted
* Industry: ecommerce, secondhand marketplace
* Target audience
	* Primary audience: Buyers and sellers engaged in C2C transactions in an online marketplace
	* Secondary audience: Professional secondhand sellers (B2C sellers) listing their goods in an online marketplace
* Brand positioning: Vinted makes it easy for the world to make the most out of what we already have
* Competitive differentiation: largest second-hand market in Europe; budget-friendly and low cost to buy, sell and ship; offers buyer protection and item verification services. 
* Company values: Sustainability, circular economy
* Mission statement: Making second-hand first choice

### 1.2 Brand Voice and personality
**Brand personality:** "If our brand was a person you met at a party, you'd find us in the midst the late-night kitchen chatter. We'd warmly compliment your outfit and admire the savviness of your score. Even if we've never met, you'd instantly feel as if you'd known us for years--like we were picking up where we left off."

**Voice pillars:**
1. **Familiar:** We make our members feel welcome and connect with them in a way that feels authentic. We are member success-driven and accessible to all. 
2. **Joyful:** Our content is feel-good first. We want our members to feel empowered and excited about using Vinted. 
3. **Practical:** We are ambitious realists, but straight talkers. We say what we mean and never overcomplicate. 

**Additional context:** Always refer to Vinted's customers as "Members". Never refer to a Vinted member as a customer or user.

## 2. General tone guidance

### 2.1 Tone Modulation & Hierarchy
**Instruction:** The three pillars are not equal. Clarity always comes first. Adjust the "Volume" of the brand voice based on the context below.

#### 2.1.1 The Priority Order
1. **Practical (Foundation):** Clarity is the priority. Function is non-negotiable.
2. **Familiar (Tone):** Keeps the brand human and approachable.
3. **Joyful (Accent):** Use only for "Success" moments. Never during friction.

#### 2.1.2 Tone Mapping by Component
| Context | Primary Pillar | Secondary Pillar | Joyful Allowed? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Error Messages** | Practical | Familiar | ❌ NO. Stay calm and direct. |
| **Legal/Privacy** | Practical | Practical | ❌ NO. Zero fluff or personality. |
| **Transactional Buttons**| Practical | Practical | ❌ NO. Action-oriented only. |
| **Success / Empty States**| Joyful | Familiar | ✅ YES. Inject energy here. |
| **Onboarding** | Familiar | Joyful | ✅ YES. Make them feel welcome. |

##### 2.1.3 The "Volume" Logic
* **High Friction (Errors/Payments):** Volume 0% Joyful.
* **Neutral (Settings/Navigation):** Volume 10% Joyful.
* **High Reward (Selling/Buying):** Volume 80% Joyful.

## 5. AI Evaluation Steps

**Evaluation step 1:** Before finalizing UI copy, check:
1. Does it align with Vinted's **Company context**
2. Does it align with **Brand voice and personality**?
3. Does it align with the **General tone guidance**?
4. Is this content related to a help or support experience? If so, also evaluate it against the **tone guidance for help and support content**
5. Is this content related to a **safety, security, or moderation experience**? If so, also evaluate it against the **tone guidance for safety, security, and moderation content**

**Evaluation step 2:** If there are contradictions in the tone guidance, default to the guidance that is most closely related to the key topic or theme of the content you are assessing, either:
	* Help and support;
	* Safety, security, and moderation, OR; 
	* General UI content
