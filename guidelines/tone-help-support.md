# Vinted voice and tone - help and support content

This tone guide explains how to modulate Vinted's general tone of voice for help and support related content. If any of the guidance here conflicts with the information outlined in Vinted voice and tone - general guidelines, differ to this document.

## Context
Adopt this tone when creating help or support-related UI content:
* Help and support content is any content asset or piece of information designed to help members how to use Vinted or resolve challenges related to using Vinted. 
* The primary goal of this type of content is to educate and inform Vinted members
* Examples of content that should use this tone include information banners, tooltips, and support-related user notices (i.e. inbox messsages that inform Vinted members about customer support decisions and outcomes). 

**Example** 

General Vinted content:
```YAML
dialog:
	Heading: Vinted is your platform for pre-owned pieces you’ll love
	Subheading: One community, thousands of brands, and a whole lot of second-hand style. Ready to get started? Here’s how it works.
```

Help-related content:

```YAML
tooltip:
	icon: info-circle-filled
	body-copy: If you created your account with Apple, Google, or Facebook, use the same method to log in.
```

## Tone modulation

Help and support content only uses two of Vinted's three brand voice principles, listed below in order of importance:
1. **Practical (Foundation):** Clarity is the priority. Function is non-negotiable. 
2. **Familiar (Tone):** Keeps the brand human and approachable.

### Additional tone principles

**Help and support content should always be:**
* Instructive and supportive
* Success-focused and outcome-oriented
* Understanding and empathetic (when appropriate)

**Help and support content should never:**
* Be pushy or salesy. Focus on guiding members towards a satisfactory solution.
* Be judgemental or condescending. Focus on helping them understand the situation, consequences, and how to overcome challenges. 
* Use jargon. Stick to simple language and get to the point, but take time to explain complex concepts when they're important to the members' goals.

**Writing voice - active vs. passive:**
* Help and support content should typically use the active voice, especially when instructing on user actions. Only use the passive voice in these situations:
	* To describe actions that are completed by the system
	* To avoid blaming the member
	* To convey an inpersonal tone, such as when describing negative consequences or disciplinary outcomes

### Tone mapping by situation
| Context | Primary Pillar | Secondary Pillar | Other characteristics|
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bad or frustrating news** | Practical | Familiar | Focus on reassuring the member. Consider acknowledging their frustration. Clearly outline the situation, consequences, and next steps. |
| **Alerts and updates** | Practical | Practical | Focus on informing the member. State facts neutrally in a polite, concise way. |
| **Warnings**| Practical | Practical | Focus on neutrally stating the facts in a sober and resolute way. Avoid making accusations or scolding. |

#### Tone "Volume" Logic
* **Bad or frustrating news:** Volume 30% Familiar.
* **Alerts and updates:** Volume 10% Familiar.
* **Warnings:** Volume 0% Familiar.
