# Info Banners — Component Rules

## Types
Info (neutral) / Success (positive) / Warning (potential issue) / Error (blocking)

## Structure
Title (optional) + Body text + CTA (optional; one only)

## Title (optional)
- 1 line. Lead with outcome or key message.
- Specific: what happened / what's at risk / what to do.
- Neutral tone: inform and guide; don't scold or dramatise.
- No end period. No exclamation marks.
- Aim ≤ 40 chars; hard max 55.
- Omit when the body alone is a clear, self-contained single line.

## Body text
- Essential clarity: impact and/or next step.
- Minimum detail needed (eligibility, deadlines, requirements).
- State required actions clearly and early.
- Use "you" when the user must act.
- No period for a single fragment. Period only if 2 sentences (max 2).
- Aim ≤ 90 chars; hard max 120.

## CTA (optional; one only)
- Only when there's a clear next step.
- 1–3 words, verb-first, specific to the action.
- Aim ≤ 12 chars.

## Type-specific guidance

**Info (neutral)**
- Helpful context or guidance without risk.
- Avoid urgent words.

**Success**
- Confirm what worked or completed.
- Body text only preferred.
- Say what succeeded ("Draft saved"), not "Success!".
- No exclamation marks.

**Warning**
- Something may go wrong if not addressed.
- State risk + consequence (briefly).
- Include safe next step in body or CTA.

**Error (blocking)**
- User can't continue or something failed.
- State what happened + how to fix it (or timeline if fix isn't immediate).
- Avoid blame ("You did…") and vague messages ("Something went wrong").
- Follow error message best practices.

## ✅ / ❌ Examples

| Type | ✅ Good | ❌ Bad | Why |
|------|---------|--------|-----|
| Info | Title: "Use only your photos" / Body: "Your listing may be hidden if it includes photos that aren't yours." | Title: "Important notice!!!" / Body: "Please follow the rules to avoid issues." / CTA: "Learn more" | ✅ Clear rule + consequence. ❌ Vague, hypey, not actionable. |
| Success | Title: "Draft saved" | Title: "Success! Your draft has been saved successfully." | ✅ Short confirmation, no redundancy. ❌ Wordy and repetitive. |
| Warning | Title: "Electronic Verification may not apply" / Body: "Bundled items aren't eligible for physical authentication." | Title: "Warning" / Body: "This could cause problems later." | ✅ Names the risk and scope. ❌ Generic and unclear. |
| Error | Title: "Your items are hidden" / Body: "Add your DAC7 info to make them visible." / CTA: "Add DAC7 info" | Title: "Error occurred" / Body: "You didn't provide DAC7. Fix it to continue" / CTA: "Continue" | ✅ Impact + fix + specific CTA; neutral tone. ❌ Blamey, vague CTA, adds friction. |
