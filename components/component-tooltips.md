# Tooltips — Component Rules

## Structure
Body text + CTA (optional; one only)
No title recommended — keep single-message.

## Body text
- Explain one thing only: what it does, or why it matters.
- Relevant to what's on screen right now.
- Assume read out of context — include the minimum noun needed ("favourites", "bundle", "offer").
- 1–2 short sentences max.
- Default display: 4 seconds — copy must be readable in that time.
- Aim ≤ 80 chars; hard max 110 (including spaces).
- Avoid line breaks; let the container wrap naturally.

## Punctuation
- Full stop for complete sentences.
- No exclamation marks.
- No parentheses or semicolons.

## CTA (optional)
- Only if there's a clear next step.
- 1–3 words, verb-first.

## ✅ / ❌ Examples

| Use case | ✅ Good | ❌ Bad | Why |
|----------|---------|--------|-----|
| Feature discovery (Favourites) | "Like this item? Add it to your favourites." | "Tap the heart icon to favourite this product!!!" | ✅ Benefit + simple action, calm tone. ❌ UI-instruction-heavy, hypey, "product" (Vinted says "items"). |
| Onboarding (Bundling) | "Buy multiple items from the same seller to save on shipping." | "Bundle now and unlock amazing savings. Limited time!" | ✅ Clear rule + value. ❌ Salesy, urgency, vague. |
| Contextual help (Offer) | "Make an offer to negotiate the price with the seller." | "Offers let you negotiate pricing and complete transactions efficiently." | ✅ Plain language, direct. ❌ Jargon, abstract, too long. |
| Lightweight tip (Filters) | "Use filters to narrow your search by size and brand." | "Filters are available on this page to refine results." | ✅ Tells what to do and why. ❌ States the obvious; low value. |
